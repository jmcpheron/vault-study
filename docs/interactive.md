# Interactive mechanism

Drag the slider. The 120-tooth ring gear rotates; the twelve 24-tooth
spur gears counter-rotate at five times the ring's angular speed
(120 ÷ 24 = 5); the twelve rack-and-pin assemblies translate radially
outward through the door body's pin bores into the frame.

## What the controls do

- **Ring rotation slider** — between −15° and +15° of ring rotation.
  Each spur counter-rotates by 5× that amount (the 5 : 1 ratio
  derived in [the gearing math page](gearing-math.html)). Each rack
  translates radially outward by `θ × r_pitch` mm, where `r_pitch` is
  the spur's pitch radius (6 mm for our 24-tooth, module-0.5 spurs).
- **Pin extension** — the live readout shows the linear distance each
  rack-and-pin assembly has moved from rest. Clamped to ±8 mm so
  extreme slider values don't push the pins off-screen.
- **Show 72 mm BCD** — swaps the spur centres from their
  gear-math-derived position (24 mm from door centre, where teeth
  actually mesh) to Adam's video-measured 72 mm bolt-circle (36 mm
  radius). At the measured BCD, the spurs are noticeably outside the
  ring's pitch circle — the discrepancy is real, and discussed in
  [the mechanics overview](mechanics.html#the-12-spur-crowding-question)
  and [`specs.md`](https://github.com/jmcpheron/vault-study/blob/main/specs.md#derived-values).

## How this page is built

The SVG above is generated by Python from the canonical parameters in
[`src/vaultkit/params.py`](https://github.com/jmcpheron/vault-study/blob/main/src/vaultkit/params.py)
plus [`src/vaultkit/gears.py`](https://github.com/jmcpheron/vault-study/blob/main/src/vaultkit/gears.py).
The generator lives in
[`src/vaultkit/explainers/schematic.py`](https://github.com/jmcpheron/vault-study/blob/main/src/vaultkit/explainers/schematic.py)
and is re-run by `vaultkit explain render`. The animation logic is
entirely client-side vanilla JavaScript in
[`docs/assets/interactive.js`](https://github.com/jmcpheron/vault-study/blob/main/docs/assets/interactive.js)
— no framework, no build step.

To regenerate after a parameter change:

```bash
vaultkit explain render
```

Then commit the regenerated `docs/_includes/mechanism-interactive.svg`.
